Research

What similar businesses do, and what I took from them

I looked at AI visibility tools, GEO agencies, fixed-price AI audits and the first pages written about Businesses in ChatGPT. I borrowed ideas, not words or branding.

  • The tools show the product first

    AI tracking tools open with a screen of what AI says about a brand, so the visitor sees the problem right away.

    Used in: B’s live answer check, C’s picture gallery and A’s marked-up answer.

  • Agencies name the assistants and lay out the steps

    The clearer agency pages name ChatGPT, Google and the others outright, then walk through a short numbered process.

    Used in: all three, as a short numbered process in plain words.

  • Fixed-price audits put price, time and contents side by side

    The best ones show what you get, how long it takes and who it’s for in one place. Many still hide the buy button away from the price.

    Used in: all three, with the PayPal button right next to the $500.

  • Almost no one sells the ChatGPT setup as a package yet

    Most coverage of Businesses in ChatGPT is blog posts. Agencies lean on client logos and big growth numbers, which we can’t honestly use yet.

    Used in: the $500 Launch leads every option. A sample finding, clearly labeled, and our working standards stand in for proof.

The same in all three

  • Prices, plan names, the PayPal link and the email address are unchanged.
  • No promises of rankings, traffic or sales, and the note that we aren’t affiliated with OpenAI.
