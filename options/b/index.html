02Why it matters

AI writes the shortlist. Three ways it goes wrong.

Assistants now sit between your business and the people looking for it. They summarize, compare and recommend using whatever they can find about you. These are the failures we test for.

best tea gift for a remote team?

  1. 1Another tea brand
  2. 2A gift-box marketplace
  3. –Your businessNot mentioned
Failure 01 · Omitted

It leaves you out

If AI can’t find clear, current facts about what you offer, it points buyers to companies it understands better.

how much is the tasting kit?

kit: loose-leaf, 6 teas
- price: $24 (old blog post)
+ price: $29 (owner confirmed)
ships: US only
Failure 02 · Incorrect

It gets the facts wrong

Old prices, discontinued products, the wrong policy or a competitor’s details can end up in the answer.

can I ship kits to 12 addresses?

The kit ships to one address per checkout. I couldn’t find details about ordering for a group.

Failure 03 · Unanswerable

It can’t answer what you never wrote down

Shipping limits, compatibility, group orders and setup steps often aren’t stated anywhere AI can read.

Can I send tasting kits to twelve colleagues at different addresses? Asked by office managers buying gifts for a team. Two assistants, three runs each.

Source gap

The product page lists kit contents, and the shipping FAQ allows one address per checkout. No page explains group orders.

Recommended fix

Confirm quantity limits, shipping options and lead times with the owner, then publish them by the order button and in the FAQ.

How we’ll measure

Rerun the same question next month and compare dated answers and cited pages. Answers vary, so we report what we observe, not guarantees.

07FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something we didn’t cover? Email nolansconsultingsolutions@gmail.com

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the work of making your business easy for AI assistants like ChatGPT and Google’s AI to find, understand and describe accurately. It focuses on the questions buyers ask AI and the sources those answers draw from.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims for a position in a list of links. GEO looks at the answer itself: whether you’re mentioned, whether the facts are right, and which sources the AI relied on. Good SEO helps, but it doesn’t tell you what AI is saying about you.

What is Businesses in ChatGPT?

A beta program OpenAI introduced in November 2026. Businesses can claim a profile in ChatGPT’s Business Hub and, once verified, update their details and see insights on mentions, website clicks and common topics. Eligible businesses can also build a business agent that answers customer questions.

Is my business eligible for a ChatGPT business agent?

Right now OpenAI offers business agents to eligible ecommerce and online-service businesses in the US. We check before we start. If you aren’t eligible, ChatGPT Launch still includes the profile setup, audit, readability check and report.

Can you guarantee we’ll show up in ChatGPT?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. AI tools decide what they show, and answers vary between runs. We promise the work: accurate setup, documented testing and clear reporting. We don’t promise rankings, recommendations, traffic or sales.

How long does ChatGPT Launch take?

It’s delivered within 7 business days after you complete verification. You complete the identity or business verification step with OpenAI yourself, and we guide you through it.

Can a ChatGPT business agent take payments or bookings?

No. Business agents answer questions and link to your website. Purchases happen on your site.

Do I have to start with ChatGPT Launch?

No. You can start with Focused GEO or Full GEO. If you start with ChatGPT Launch and move to Focused GEO within 30 days, the full $500 comes off your first month.

What do you need from us?

Your website, the products or services that matter most, and someone who can confirm facts and approve changes. A public website link is enough to start.

Are you affiliated with OpenAI or Google?

No. Nolan’s Consulting is independent and isn’t affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by OpenAI, Google or any other AI company.
