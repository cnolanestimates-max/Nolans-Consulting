The punch list we usually find

AI tools build their answers from what's on the web. When your website is thin, old or vague, they guess. These are the guesses that cost you calls.

What AI gets wrongWhy it happensWhat we fix
Mixes you up with a different company Your name, city and trade aren't stated clearly in one place, so a similar name wins. One clear About section, and the same company details on your site, Google Business Profile and LinkedIn.
Says you don't do work you do The service lives on your bid forms and in your head, not on your website. A plain page for each service: what you do, where, and what size jobs you take.
