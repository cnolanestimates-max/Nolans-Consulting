When AI search gets you wrong, buyers move on

AI answers are built from what's on the web. When your pages are unclear, out of date or silent on a question, the answer goes to whoever explains it better.

  • It confuses you with another company

    The answer mixes up your name, repeats outdated service details or misstates who you serve.

  • A real buying question has no answer

    Compatibility, service area, setup steps or ordering limits aren't clearly stated on your site.

  • Competitors are easier to understand

    Their pages explain differences, evidence and limits more clearly, so they get cited and you don't.

  • You have screenshots but no process

    Your team has seen odd AI answers, but nothing tells you what to change or whether a change worked.

How the work runs each month

Hands-on, documented and repeatable. You approve every change before it goes live.

  1. Agree on the questions

    We confirm your priority offer, your buyers, and the exact questions and AI search experiences to test.

  2. Record the evidence

    We run each question and save the date, the answer, the cited sources and screenshots.

  3. Find the source problem

    For each finding we identify the page or missing fact behind it, ranked by how much it matters to buyers.

  4. Make approved changes

    We draft clear page updates, you confirm the facts, and the changes are published and checked.

  5. Retest and report

    We ask the same questions again and report what improved, what didn't and what to do next.

Sample audit, fictional example

A missing answer at the point of purchase

A specialty tea company sells tasting kits. An office manager wants to send kits to twelve colleagues at different addresses, and asks AI search how.

Observed answer

The AI answer sends the buyer to individual checkout and offers no way to confirm a group order.

Source gap

The product page lists the kit contents, and the shipping FAQ allows one address per checkout. Neither explains group ordering.

Proposed edit

Have the owner confirm quantities, shipping options and lead time, then publish those details beside the ordering button.

What we would measure

Repeat the question in both tools, save dated responses and cited pages, and compare accuracy, noting that answers vary between runs.
